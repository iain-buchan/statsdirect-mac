# Published examination examples used for inspiration

Reviewed 25 September 2026. No official question stems, answer banks or images are distributed with the app. The 30 draft MCQs are original and require subject-expert review. Four shared items put epidemiology and causal reasoning into every pathway. They are brief teaching sets, not validated examinations.

| Source | What was accessible | Application in this build |
|---|---|---|
| [MRCP(UK) Part 1 sample examination](https://www.thefederation.uk/examinations/part-1/part-1-sample-questions) and [exam description](https://www.thefederation.uk/examinations/part-1) | Official access and best-of-five format verified. The mock requests registration or acceptance steps; its gated questions were not read. | Link to the official resource; no claim to have reproduced its questions. |
| [Published MRCP-style diagnostic questions](https://www.pastpaperhero.com/resources/rcp-mrcp-statistics-epidemiology-and-evidence-based-medicine-sensitivity-specificity-and-predictive-values?content=article) | Public third-party questions and explanations; this is not an official Royal College bank. | Topic inspiration for original diagnostic-threshold and likelihood-ratio items. General statistical reasoning was independently checked. |
| [RCGP November 2025 sample answers](https://www.rcgp.org.uk/getmedia/dca96621-2599-4e6e-9a99-7a1ab2affff9/AKT-Example-Questions-(with-answers)-November-2025.pdf) | Questions 3, 10, 20, 24 and 41 concern data interpretation. | Original referral-rate and confidence-interval examples. Explicitly distinguish effect magnitude from statistical evidence; do not rank significance by effect size alone. |
| [FPH DFPH specimen listing](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-diplomate-examination-dfph/the-diplomate-examination-dfph-preparation/), [Paper B and marking scheme](https://www.fph.org.uk/media/jp0owtuj/specimen-paper-b-questions-and-mark-scheme.pdf), [Paper IIA specimens](https://www.fph.org.uk/media/3017/new-paper-iia-specimen-questions.pdf) | Official indexed excerpts cover service distributions/case mix (Paper B Q1), screening and critical appraisal. Direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403, so only accessible indexed sections were examined. | Original case-mix and lead-time-bias items; a conversational explanation exercise. DFPH written questions are not relabelled as official MCQs. |
| [FPH final membership assessment](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-faculty-of-public-health-final-membership-examination/marking-results-and-feedback/) | Official description of competencies across six stations. | The tutor distinguishes MFPH practical communication from DFPH written appraisal and MCQ practice. No simulated score is an official examination grade. |

Learners can open these links through **Exam examples & sources**. Examination bodies and the independent publisher have not endorsed this feature. Sources inform topics and format, not a promise of blueprint coverage or exam success. Subject experts should review wording, keys, distractors, causal assumptions and educational level before any formal pilot.
