Statistical teaching checks:
- Establish the unit of observation, matching, independence/clustering, denominator, outcome
  direction and time horizon before choosing a method. Different patients do not automatically
  imply independent data. A clinic or time label alone does not establish pairing.
- For small-sample paired t inference, normality concerns differences and independence is
  between pairs. Inspect outliers and design; a non-significant normality test does not prove
  normality. Do not use a normality P value as an automatic method-selection rule.
- SE(mean) = population SD/sqrt(n) assumes independent observations from a common population
  with finite variance. Sample SD and estimated SE vary; bigger samples do not cure bias.
  Separate SD, SE, confidence intervals for parameters and prediction intervals for outcomes.
- State the null model and assumptions for P values. They are not probabilities that the null
  is true or that chance caused a result. Nominal confidence coverage depends on assumptions
  and method; exact discrete intervals can be conservative. Non-significance is not equivalence.
- Keep risk differences and their intervals in the same group order. Label adverse versus
  desirable outcomes before calling a ratio beneficial. NNT uses people with an outcome and a
  time horizon, not repeated-event counts. If the risk-difference interval crosses zero, NNT
  uncertainty spans infinity and both benefit and harm, not one ordinary finite interval.
- Define diagnostic denominators and reference diagnosis. Prevalence-based predictive-value
  examples hold sensitivity/specificity fixed; applicability can change with case mix and
  threshold. Multiply pre-test odds by a likelihood ratio, then convert back to probability.
- Separate first-event risks, person-time incidence rates and prevalence. Interpret case-control
  odds ratios in light of control sampling and analysis, not an automatic rare-disease rule.
- Do not diagnose confounding from a change after adjustment alone, or infer causality from
  equal adjusted risks. Justify adjustment using causal knowledge; mediator adjustment alone
  does not identify a direct causal effect. Describe effect modification on a specified scale.
  Selection on a collider can, but need not always, induce association.
- Service-level associations do not establish individual-level effects. Conventional exact
  small-sample linear-regression inference models independent, constant-variance normal errors
  about a linear conditional mean; it does not require normally distributed predictors.
- Consider multiplicity, selective follow-up, measurement error and screening lead-time bias.
  Give the independence assumption for 1-(1-alpha)^m. A difference in significance between
  studies or subgroups is not itself a significant difference between their effects.
